01 · The quiet promise

Koble is made for personal, hands-on photo play. You can open the app without an account, password, username, or social profile. The core studio experience is designed to work on your device, with your photographs and creative decisions staying close to you.

This policy explains the information Koble handles when you use its rooms, import a photo, paint light, save a keepsake, buy lumens, or export a sun-print. It also explains the choices available in iOS settings. We use plain words because privacy should feel as understandable as the controls in the darkroom.

The short version: Koble does not require an account, does not upload your selected photographs to a Koble server, and does not read your whole Photos library. You choose what enters the canvas and what leaves it.

02 · Photographs, canvases, and exports

When you choose a photograph

If you use Bring Your Own Photo, iOS presents the system photo picker. Koble receives the image you select so it can create a temporary working copy for the canvas. Koble does not scan your library, browse unrelated albums, or collect a catalog of your other images.

The selected working copy is used on the device for the current painting flow. Koble stores the path needed to replay a keepsake while that local data remains available. You can remove a keepsake from the Lightbox; this removes its Koble record. The original image in Photos is not changed by painting and is not removed by deleting a keepsake.

What Koble saves locally

A keepsake can include a title, creation time, scene reference, local source path, painted stroke positions, selected tools, colors, intensity, and recipe information. Crafted recipes and favorites can also be stored locally. These records exist so the Lightbox can show your work again without an account.

When you make a sun-print

After you choose Sun-print to Photos and approve the iOS permission, Koble renders the composition and asks Photos to add the finished image. Koble does not need permission to delete or reorganize your existing library for this action. The saved image is then managed by the Photos app and your device backup choices.

03 · Device settings and local signals

Preferences and local balance

Koble stores studio preferences such as film grain, brush intensity, soft focus, haptic whisper, crafted recipes, favorites, keepsakes, and the local lumen balance using app storage on your device. These values help Koble remember how your studio feels. They are not an account profile and are not sent to a Koble server.

Permissions

Koble may ask for permission to access selected photos, add sun-prints to Photos, or use the camera when a capture feature is presented. You can decline a permission. Photo importing and printing will not work without the relevant permission, but other rooms can remain available. You can change permissions later in iOS Settings.

Tracking choice

Depending on the app version and device state, iOS may show a tracking authorization prompt. Koble's editing tools do not depend on accepting that prompt. If you decline, the app should continue to provide its core creative experience. Koble does not ask you to create a profile in order to personalize your studio.

Temporary files and deletion

Photo selections may be converted into a temporary JPEG working file so the canvas can use them consistently. Temporary files are kept in the app's temporary area and are subject to normal iOS cleanup. Removing a local keepsake or removing Koble from your device removes Koble's local records according to iOS behavior. Photos already exported to your library remain there until you manage them in Photos.

04 · StoreKit purchases and lumens

Koble offers consumable lumen packs through Apple's StoreKit purchase system. When you begin a purchase, Apple handles the payment sheet, storefront, payment method, receipt, and transaction process. Koble receives the product and transaction information needed to identify the selected pack and deliver its matching lumens.

Koble verifies the StoreKit transaction before adding lumens to the local purse. Verified transactions are finished after delivery. Pending, cancelled, failed, or unverified transactions are not treated as completed purchases. Koble does not receive your full payment card number, bank details, or Apple Account password.

Purchase history and restoration

Lumen packs are consumable. The local purse is designed for the device and app installation where the purchase is delivered. Apple may retain transaction records according to Apple's own account and purchase settings. For questions about a charge, payment method, or Apple receipt, use Apple's purchase support channels; for a pack that was verified but did not appear in Koble, contact us at koble7Qm2@outlook.com with the relevant Apple support information.

No account means no profile

Koble does not combine a purchase with a Koble username, email profile, advertising profile, or social identity. The app uses the information made available by StoreKit only to complete the purchase flow and credit the local purse.

05 · Your choices and control panel

You decide which photograph enters the canvas, whether to grant Photos access, whether to grant camera access when offered, whether to allow tracking, which preferences to use, which keepsakes to keep, and whether to export a frame.

  • Choose less: select only the photo you want to edit through the system picker.
  • Change permissions: open iOS Settings, find Koble, and adjust Photos, Camera, or tracking choices when those controls are available.
  • Remove local work: delete keepsakes and favorites inside Koble, or remove the app to clear its local storage.
  • Keep originals safe: use Photos or your own backup for photographs and exports you cannot replace.
  • Ask us: request a plain explanation of how a feature handles information by emailing the contact below.

Children and family devices

Koble is a creative photo tool, not a service built to collect information from children. Because no account is required, the app does not ask for a child's name, birth date, school, or contact details. Parents and guardians can manage device permissions and StoreKit purchase controls through iOS family settings.

Security in practical terms

Koble keeps its main creative records on the device and relies on iOS protections for app storage, Photos permissions, and StoreKit transactions. No local storage method can promise that a device will never be lost, damaged, compromised, or restored. Use a device passcode and keep personal backups where appropriate.

06 · Questions, updates, and contact

If you believe Koble handled a photo unexpectedly, a local record did not behave as described, a purchase is missing after verification, or you simply want to understand a feature, email koble7Qm2@outlook.com.

When you contact us, share only what is needed to investigate. A useful message can include your app version, device model, iOS version, the room or feature involved, and the approximate time of the issue. Please do not send payment card numbers, passwords, or private photographs unless you intentionally decide that an image is necessary.

Koble may update this policy when the app adds a room, changes a permission flow, introduces a new purchase path, or changes how local information is handled. The date at the top shows when this page was last refreshed. We will keep the description aligned with the Koble experience so you can make an informed choice each time you return to the studio.

Thank you for treating photographs, people, and your own creative traces with care.
