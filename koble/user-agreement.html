01 · Welcome to the darkroom

Koble is a small, private-feeling creative studio for painting lens flares, film leaks, halos, streaks, ghost chains, and bokeh dust over photographs. These pages describe the shared rhythm of using Koble. They are written so you can understand what the app does, what stays yours, and what happens when you use a feature.

By opening Koble, you are choosing to use it in a thoughtful way. You keep control of the photographs you bring in, the marks you paint, the recipes you make, and the frames you choose to keep. Koble does not require a username, password, profile, or social account to begin.

The short version: use photographs you are allowed to use, treat other people and their images with care, review a frame before you save or share it, and contact us when something feels unclear.

02 · Access without an account

Koble is designed to open directly into the studio. You can use the Today room, Darkroom, Recipes, Lightbox, Quiet room, Guidebook, and About pages without creating an account or signing in.

Your device is your studio key

Because there is no account system, Koble recognizes your work through the app installation and the device where it was created. Preferences such as grain veil, brush glow, soft focus, haptic whisper, crafted recipes, favorites, keepsakes, and your local lumen balance are stored for the app on that device.

This also means that Koble cannot automatically restore a local collection to a new device through a profile. Keep exported images and any personal backups you want to preserve. Removing the app may remove locally stored Koble data, while images already saved to Photos remain in Photos according to your device settings.

Keeping the studio usable

You agree to provide a compatible device and a current enough operating system for the version of Koble you use. You are responsible for keeping your device passcode, operating system, and personal backups in good condition.

03 · Your photographs and painted light

Koble gives you tools rather than finished answers. You can select a photo from your library, choose a procedural practice scene, paint by touch, adjust hue and intensity, soften a composition, undo a stroke, clear a frame, save a keepsake to your Lightbox, and create a sun-print for your Photos library.

What remains yours

Your source photographs, hand-painted strokes, custom recipes, titles, and finished creative choices remain yours. Koble does not claim ownership of them. You decide whether and where to share a finished image.

Permission to use what you bring

Only import or edit photographs that you have permission to use. If a photograph includes another person, private space, artwork, or material belonging to someone else, consider whether you have the appropriate permission before sharing the result. Koble cannot determine those permissions for you.

Saving and exporting

A keepsake is a local creative record inside Koble. A sun-print is an exported image written to your Photos library after you approve the Photos permission request. Exporting does not publish an image to Koble, and Koble does not create a public gallery or social feed.

04 · The lumen purse and purchases

Koble uses lumens as its in-app creative material. New installations receive the welcome amount shown in the app. Lumens can be spent on sun-prints and may be used by future creative features. Your current balance is shown in the Lumen Till and in places where a sun-print is prepared.

Buying a light pack

When you select a pack, Apple presents the purchase sheet through StoreKit. The price shown by Apple is the price that applies at that moment. A successful purchase adds the matching lumen amount after the transaction is verified. Cancelled, pending, failed, or unverified transactions do not add lumens.

Apple handles payment authorization, payment method details, receipts, and the purchase experience. Koble does not ask you to type payment card details into the app. Consumable lumen packs are intended to be used as shown in the product description. Keep your Apple purchase records for your own reference.

Purchase interruptions

A purchase may be pending because of device settings, family approval, connectivity, storefront availability, or Apple processing. Do not repeatedly tap the purchase control while it is processing. Koble listens for completed transactions and adds verified purchases when Apple delivers them to the app.

05 · Good care in the studio

Please use Koble in a way that protects people, photographs, and the calm of the creative space. Do not use it to create or distribute content that is threatening, abusive, deceptive, exploitative, hateful, or intended to invade another person’s private life.

Do not attempt to interfere with the app, bypass a purchase, manipulate a transaction, reverse-engineer private services, overload a device or network, or use automated activity to disrupt Koble or Apple services. Do not present Koble-made images as documentary evidence when they have been artistically altered.

Respectful sharing

Koble is built for personal making and careful sharing. If you send a frame to someone, make sure the context is honest. A painted flare can change the feeling of a photograph; that is part of the art, and it is worth being clear about when the difference matters.

When the app cannot promise perfection

Creative software can encounter device limits, unavailable photos, interrupted exports, operating system changes, or temporary StoreKit and Photos issues. Koble aims to keep your work local and usable, but you should keep copies of important originals and finished images outside the app.

06 · Updates, quiet exits, and questions

Koble may grow new rooms, brushes, recipes, accessibility improvements, export options, or purchase packs. An update may change a visual detail or retire a feature when that is needed for compatibility, safety, or a better studio experience. We will keep the in-app description of a feature aligned with what it does.

You can stop using Koble at any time. You can remove local keepsakes, favorites, recipes, and app data using the controls available in the app or by removing the app from your device. Images already exported to Photos are managed there and are not removed simply because Koble is removed.

Talk to the studio

If a purchase does not arrive, a frame cannot be saved, a permission behaves unexpectedly, or you have a question about these pages, write to koble7Qm2@outlook.com. Please include the app version, device model, operating system, and a calm description of what happened. Never send passwords, payment card numbers, or private photographs unless you have intentionally chosen to do so.

These pages describe the Koble experience in plain language. The latest version shown in the app is the version to follow when the studio changes.
